## Digital Conversion Prompts
These prompts were used to generate polished digital versions from the sketches:

### 1. Mushroom-cap creature
```
Highly detailed digital creature art of a whimsical yet slightly menacing plant monster. Triangular green mushroom-like cap head with textured leafy surface, two large white eyes with dark pupils looking sideways, wide red smiling mouth, black spiky fringe lines around the mouth like teeth or gills. Tall slender green stem, base of broad green leaves with white highlights. Realistic organic textures, subtle wet sheen, soft studio lighting, clean white background, professional creature concept art style, vibrant greens and red accents.
```

### 2. Pitcher-plant creature
```
Highly detailed digital creature art of an aggressive plant monster. Large bulbous green pitcher-plant body with smooth glossy texture and subtle highlights. On top, a white-and-green face with angry narrowed eyes, furrowed brow, and a huge open red mouth lined with white ridged teeth. Small pointed green leaves protruding from the sides. Realistic organic materials, wet highlights, dramatic lighting, clean white background, professional creature concept art style.
```

### 3. Multi-legged flower-headed creature
```
Highly detailed digital creature art of a strange multi-limbed plant monster. Central green body with a dark spotted chest area. Two large red flower-like heads with white eyes and green centers stacked on top. Multiple long green root-like or tentacle legs extending outward, some with leaf shapes. Small wing-like green leaves on the upper body. Realistic textured plant surfaces, subtle moss and vein details, soft lighting, clean white background, professional creature concept art style.
```
